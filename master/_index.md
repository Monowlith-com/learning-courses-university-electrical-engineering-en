# Course overview – Electrical Engineering {#kurseinstieg}

This university-level self-study course covers electrical engineering, from mathematical and physical foundations to installations, machines, power electronics, control and electronics. It is intended for learners with upper-secondary mathematics and basic physics. The pathway combines bachelor foundations, elective specialisations, bachelor–master bridges and master research projects. All planned lessons are present; final review is pending. The course does not substitute for an accredited degree, supervised laboratory work or professional electrical qualifications.
